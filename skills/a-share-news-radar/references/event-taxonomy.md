# Event Taxonomy And Reasoning Rules

Use this reference to classify news and choose the right impact chain. A catalyst is useful only when it has a plausible path to A-share revenue, costs, supply, policy support, risk appetite, or market attention.

## Scoring Dimensions

Score each dimension from 0 to 5 unless a script asks for 0-10:

- Fact strength: confirmed official data, company filing, or authoritative media beats market rumors.
- Industry distance: direct product/order/cost exposure beats loose concept association.
- Earnings relevance: catalysts tied to revenue, margin, capacity, or commodity price beat pure sentiment.
- Policy persistence: formal policy, regulation, or inspection cycle beats one-day headlines.
- Crowding risk: heavily chased themes score lower when the best reaction may already be priced in.
- Counterevidence risk: conflicting facts, weak scale, valuation pressure, or demand weakness lowers priority.

## Event Types

### AI And Technology Releases

Triggers: major model, chip, robotics, autonomous driving, satellite, semiconductor, or application release.

Reasoning paths:

- Product release -> demand for compute/chips/servers/cooling/optics/software applications.
- Breakthrough demo -> related domestic substitute or application theme.
- Commercial order -> direct supplier and component makers.

Risk checks:

- Is the A-share company actually a supplier or only sharing a concept label?
- Is revenue contribution disclosed or material?
- Has the theme already had consecutive sharp gains?

Historical calibration:

- Spring Festival Gala humanoid robot dance -> Unitree/robotics attention -> humanoid robot parts and controller themes became active.
- Google Nano Banana, Jimeng, SeedDance, and similar visual AI releases -> AI application and visual-content themes, but most A-share mappings require revenue verification.

### Exploding IP, Film, Game, Variety Show, Or Consumer Brand

Triggers: film box office surprise, viral toy/IP, game hit, cultural event, celebrity-brand event, **variety show trending on social media (微博热搜/抖音热榜/综艺霸榜)**.

Reasoning paths:

- Hit content -> producer/distributor/cinema/advertising/derivatives.
- Viral IP -> IP owner, licensed merchandise, packaging, retail, marketing, logistics.
- Supply shortage -> capacity expansion, manufacturing partners, channel partners.

Risk checks:

- Distinguish direct owner from peripheral concept stocks.
- Check box office/revenue recognition period and company announcement.
- Viral popularity can fade quickly; flag "emotion-driven" names.
- **For variety show/IP trending events**: identify the platform/platform operator (平台运营方) first — this is usually the most direct beneficiary. Check if the company owns the platform, produces the content, or only has loose concept linkage.
- Verify the IP/platform relationship through company announcements or official channel disclosures, not just forum speculation.
- Track whether the buzz is still rising or peaking — entering at the peak has different risk than entering during the ascent.

Historical calibration:

- Ne Zha 2 -> film box office -> Light Chaser/Enlight Media and broader media attention.
- Labubu/Pop Mart -> IP economy and supply-chain attention -> A-share candidates need confirmation of actual cooperation.

## Additional Calibration Cases (Variety Show / Trending IP)

- 乘风破浪的姐姐 (Mango TV) -> viewer engagement spike -> Mango Ultra Media (芒果超媒) streaming metrics attention, but revenue linkage needs verification through quarterly reports.
- 种地吧 (Mango TV) -> youth culture resonance -> Mango Ultra Media subscriber metrics; check whether traffic translates to subscription growth.
- 热门综艺热搜霸榜 -> streaming platform operators, cast agencies, brand sponsors -> differentiate between direct platform owners vs. peripheral concept stocks.

### Policy, Meetings, And Government Work

Triggers: Two Sessions, Central Economic Work Conference, Politburo meeting, ministry policy, local subsidy, industrial plan, safety inspection, procurement rules.

Reasoning paths:

- Policy language -> supported sectors -> budget/subsidy/procurement beneficiaries.
- Regulation tightening -> compliance leaders benefit, non-compliant capacity exits.
- State investment -> infrastructure/equipment/software/local SOEs.

Risk checks:

- Separate policy wording from funded implementation.
- Identify whether the policy is incremental or already expected.
- Local policy usually has narrower impact than national policy.

### Geopolitical Conflict And War

Triggers: war, sanctions, shipping lane risk, military escalation, diplomatic break.

Reasoning paths:

- Conflict risk -> gold, oil, defense, drones, shipping, energy security.
- Oil price rise -> upstream oil benefit, chemical/airline cost pressure.
- Shipping disruption -> freight rates and shipbuilding/shipping attention.

Risk checks:

- Fast-moving headlines can reverse.
- Commodity moves may already reflect the news.
- A-share impact depends on import dependence and pricing power.

### Tariffs, Trade Frictions, And Diplomatic Restrictions

Triggers: tariff implementation, export control, import ban, anti-dumping, sanctions, bilateral tension.

Reasoning paths:

- Tariff lands after fear phase -> affected exporters may rebound if uncertainty is removed.
- Import ban -> domestic substitution and alternative suppliers.
- Export control -> domestic equipment/material substitution, but also demand risk.

Risk checks:

- Determine whether the event is new, paused, delayed, or already priced.
- Check which companies have affected revenue exposure.
- Beware "benefit" that is actually demand destruction.

Historical calibration:

- China suspended imports of Japanese aquatic products in 2023 -> domestic aquatic products and substitutes were watched.

### Holiday, Seasonality, And Consumption

Triggers: Spring Festival, National Day, winter season, summer travel, school season, shopping festivals.

Reasoning paths:

- Holiday -> travel, airlines, hotels, tourism, cinema, catering, duty-free.
- Winter -> ice/snow economy, heating, coal/gas, outdoor apparel.
- National Day / Spring Festival -> film box office and passenger traffic.

Risk checks:

- Seasonal expectations are often pre-traded.
- Compare actual booking/box-office/passenger data with expectations.

### Supply Disruption, Accidents, And Safety Inspections

Triggers: mine accident, plant fire, port closure, natural disaster, disease outbreak, environmental inspection.

Reasoning paths:

- Accident -> safety checks -> supply restriction -> price rise -> upstream producers benefit.
- Raw-material shortage -> downstream margin pressure unless pass-through is strong.
- Inspection cycle -> compliant leaders and safety equipment.

Risk checks:

- Assess geographic and capacity scale.
- Check whether demand is strong enough to absorb higher prices.
- Watch for quick resumption, imports, or policy-driven supply protection.

Historical calibration:

- Shanxi Qinyuan Liushenyu coal mine accident on May 22, 2026 -> safety inspection expectations -> coking coal/coke futures and coal themes reacted, but institutions warned of pulse-like moves and demand constraints.

### Commodity And Macro Rotation

Triggers: sharp moves in gold, silver, oil, copper, coal, FX, rates, inflation, or liquidity.

Reasoning paths:

- Precious metals rise -> gold/silver miners and jewelry hedges.
- Oil rise -> upstream oil and oilfield services; downstream chemicals and airlines can suffer.
- Tech overheat / risk-off -> defensive commodities and high-dividend assets may attract attention.

Risk checks:

- Commodity equities can lag or diverge from futures.
- Currency, hedging, and cost structure matter.
- Do not assume rotation purely because another sector rose too much; require price or flow evidence.

## Confidence Labels

- High: confirmed event, direct A-share linkage, plausible earnings or policy impact, limited contradiction.
- Medium: confirmed event but linkage or magnitude needs verification.
- Low: mostly sentiment mapping, rumor-like, distant linkage, or already crowded.
