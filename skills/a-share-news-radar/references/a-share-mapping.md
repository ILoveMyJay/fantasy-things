# A-Share Theme Mapping Seed List

Use this file as a starting map, not as proof. Before naming a stock in a daily report, verify current listing status, business relevance, announcement language, and whether the ticker still fits the theme.

Do not limit candidate discovery to this file. For each event, search current sources for:

- Newly listed companies, renamed tickers, delisted names, suspensions, restructuring, or business pivots.
- Company announcements, annual/interim reports, investor-relations replies, exchange filings, official procurement/order disclosures, and credible industry articles.
- Market-sector pages that show current constituent stocks, but treat them as clue lists rather than proof.

If current evidence conflicts with this seed list, current evidence wins. Add newer verified candidates to the report and explicitly drop stale names.

Mark every candidate as one of:

- Direct benefit: disclosed business exposure or clear commodity/industry sensitivity.
- Indirect benefit: plausible but less direct supply-chain or substitute exposure.
- Watch/verify: concept linkage exists but needs current confirmation.
- Possible loser: margins, demand, or policy exposure may worsen.

Evidence labels used in reports:

- 公告确认: company announcement, filing, annual/interim report, exchange disclosure, or official procurement/order source confirms the link.
- 业务归属: the company's main business naturally maps to the catalyst, such as coal producers to coal prices or airlines to jet fuel costs.
- 产业链推断: plausible supplier/customer/component logic, but revenue share, customer, or order data still needs verification.
- 情绪映射: concept or market-attention mapping with weak business proof.
- 负面暴露: the event may hurt revenue, margins, demand, supply, or valuation.
- 剔除: stale, delisted, suspended, business pivoted away, or no current A-share relevance.
